1. Cloud Database Architecture & Hosting Location

The PostgreSQL database for Genius Tuition Classes is 100% cloud-hosted in an enterprise-grade cloud environment (not running locally on your laptop or machine).

Infrastructure Parameter Hosting Specification
Cloud Database Provider Supabase Enterprise Cloud
Underlying Infrastructure Amazon Web Services (AWS)
Cloud Datacenter Region ap-southeast-2 (Asia Pacific / Sydney)
Connection Pool Host aws-1-ap-southeast-2.pooler.supabase.com:5432
Database Protocol PostgreSQL 15+ (SSL Encrypted Connection)
Availability & Uptime 24/7 Cloud Hosting (Operates independently when laptop is turned off)
Database Storage & Persistence
All admissions inquiries submitted by students or parents from Koradi/Nagpur are saved permanently into the cloud database tables and reflected live in the Admin Management Portal.

Active Database Tables

  • genius_inquiries — Records student names, parent phone numbers, grade (5th–12th), subjects, preferred batch, notes, status, and timestamps.
  • genius_settings — Dynamic institute configuration: Akram Sir's name, contact numbers, Koradi address, visiting hours, and announcement alerts.
  • genius_classes — Batch offerings, curriculum (SSC & CBSE), batch caps (15–18 students), and timings.
  • genius_teachers — Educator credentials, qualifications, experience, and Akram Sir's profile.
  • genius_testimonials — Student board exam marks (e.g. 96.4%, 98/100 in Maths) and parent reviews.
  • genius_faqs — Frequently asked questions regarding offline tuition in Koradi.

3. Step-by-Step Administrator Tutorial

Step 1: Logging into the Admin Management Panel

  1. Navigate to https://genius-tuition-classes.vercel.app/admin on any phone, tablet, or desktop browser.
  2. Enter the 4-digit PIN: 2026 (or full password akramsir2026).
  3. Click "Unlock Admin Panel". The dashboard will load your live cloud data automatically.

Step 2: Managing Admission Inquiries (Leads Inbox)

The Admission Inquiries tab tracks every lead submitted through the public website:

  • Reviewing Leads: See the student's name, grade, subjects of interest, batch timing preference, notes, and exact date/time submitted.
  • 1-Click Call Parent: Tap the "Call" button to immediately dial the parent's phone number.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Parent: Tap the "WhatsApp" button. The portal will automatically open a pre-drafted chat with the parent from Akram Sir:
    "Hello! This is Akram Sir from Genius Tuition Classes, Koradi. Thank you for your inquiry for [Student Name] ([Grade]). When can we schedule your 2-day demo class?"
  • Lead Status Tracking: Update the dropdown between New, Contacted, Admitted, or Archived to stay organized during peak admission season.

Step 3: Editing Institute Details & Contact Numbers

Under the Institute & Contact Info tab, Akram Sir or the administrator can modify:

  • Institute Name & Contact Person: Change display name or director designation.
  • Display Phone & Raw WhatsApp Number: Updates all clickable call and WhatsApp links across the entire website instantly.
  • Koradi Center Address: Update landmark directions or street numbers.
  • Visiting Hours: Adjust morning (e.g. 8:00 AM – 12:30 PM) and evening (e.g. 4:00 PM – 8:30 PM) hours.
  • Announcement Banner: Update promotional alerts (e.g. "New Batch for Class 10th Starting Monday!").
  • Click "Save Changes to Database". Changes persist permanently in PostgreSQL.

Step 4: Batches, Teachers & Testimonials

  • Batches Tab: Review active curriculums (State Board SSC / CBSE), timings, and enforce the 15–18 student per batch cap.
  • Faculty Tab: Manage Akram Sir's bio, educator qualifications, and subject specializations.
  • Results Tab: Review and showcase top board marks (e.g., 96.4%, 94.8%, 98 in Maths) to build parent trust in Koradi.

4. Summary of Website Features & Marketing Focus

  • Marketing Only / Offline Coaching: The website clearly communicates that all teaching is 100% in-person at the Koradi center, eliminating student screen fatigue and online distractions.
  • Photorealistic Education Imagery: Real study desk book stack (Maths, Science, English, Social Studies), Akram Sir teaching at the whiteboard, and modern offline classroom setups.
  • Dual Lead Generation: Direct website form submission + direct 1-click WhatsApp messaging.
